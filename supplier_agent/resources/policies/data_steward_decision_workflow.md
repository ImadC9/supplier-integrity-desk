# Example Data Steward Decision Workflow

> Example workflow bundled with Supplier Integrity Desk. It is fictional — replace it with your organisation's own procedure (see `SUPPLIER_DESK_POLICY_DIR`).

## 1. Case preparation

The investigation case must show the supplier, candidate records, deterministic evidence, activity context, applicable policy passages, and citations before review begins.

## 2. Allowed outcomes

The permitted review outcomes are confirm duplicate, not duplicate, and needs investigation. A recommendation from an agent is advisory and cannot substitute for human approval.

## 3. Decision and audit record

Only a human Data Steward may approve a duplicate decision. The audit record must retain the reviewer outcome, supporting evidence, policy citations, and timestamp. The agent cannot execute a merge or delete operation.

## 4. Follow-up action

Any approved consolidation is performed later through an authorized master-data process with appropriate separation of duties. Supplier Integrity Desk does not perform that process.
