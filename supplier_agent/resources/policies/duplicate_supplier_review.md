# Example Duplicate Supplier Review Policy

> Example policy bundled with Supplier Integrity Desk. It is fictional — replace it with your organisation's own policy (see `SUPPLIER_DESK_POLICY_DIR`).

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how potential duplicate supplier records are investigated without automatically changing supplier master data.

## 2. Review principle

A similarity score is a prioritization signal, not proof of duplication. The investigator must consider identifiers, names, addresses, company scope, supplier activity, and supporting documentation.

## 3. Strong duplicate indicators

The same country-scoped tax identifier, association number, or bank account is a strong duplicate indicator. More than one matching identifier increases the urgency of review. A Data Steward must validate the source records and approve the final decision; the agent must never merge or delete supplier records automatically.

## 4. Conflicting evidence

Conflicting countries, legal entities, or active purchasing relationships require further investigation. The Data Steward must record why the records are separate suppliers or why consolidation is appropriate.
